About Me

Who I Am

I'm a Data Science student at the Institute of Business Management (IoBM) in Karachi, with over 6 years of professional experience in sales and business development behind me. That background sharpened my communication, problem-solving and client-focused thinking — and it's why I care whether a model actually solves the business problem.

As a Data Science Intern at Agency Partner Interactive, I'm building the IPR digitization platform: a Streamlit application that turns messy handwritten medical forms into structured, validated data. It runs a Qwen3-VL vision model locally through Ollama, scores every extracted field for confidence, forces a human to verify anything below an admin-configurable threshold, then writes a clean record straight into Salesforce over the REST API.

Alongside that I ship SolanaGuard — an open-source Solana transaction risk-analysis stack (API, SDK, CLI, dashboard) — DocMind, a local-first TypeScript monorepo for document intelligence (ingestion, RAG with citations, deterministic decisions, Postgres/pgvector, Docker) — and a full-stack project estimation tool for consulting workflows (AI scope extraction, role-based effort, profitability and resource calculators). I also build predictive models — including a 3-day market direction classifier using an XGBoost + LightGBM ensemble — and AI voice agents, such as an appointment setter that qualifies leads and books calls on its own.

I'm available for freelance work and can deliver AI automation of almost any kind: voice agents, chatbots, document processing, data pipelines and workflow integrations that remove manual steps.
