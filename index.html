01 — About

Technical depth, business instinct.

I'm a Data Science student at the Institute of Business Management (IoBM) in Karachi with over six years of professional experience in sales and business development. That background shapes how I build: requirements first, measurable outcomes, and systems a team can actually run.

At Agency Partner Interactive I'm building an IPR digitization platform that reads handwritten medical forms with a Qwen3-VL vision model running locally through Ollama, scores every field for confidence, gates low-confidence values behind human review and writes verified records straight into Salesforce.

Outside of that I ship open-source tooling — SolanaGuard, DocMind, a project estimation platform and a tournament game-AI bot — plus gradient-boosted market models and AI voice agents for clients.
