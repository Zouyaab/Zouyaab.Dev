About Me

Who I Am

I'm a data scientist focused on applied machine learning — making models useful inside real workflows, not just inside a notebook.

My current work is the Affinity Core IPR digitization platform: a Streamlit application that turns messy handwritten medical forms into structured, validated data. It runs a Qwen3-VL vision model locally through Ollama, scores every extracted field for confidence, forces a human to verify anything below an admin-configurable threshold, then writes a clean record straight into Salesforce over the REST API.

Alongside that I build predictive models — including a 3-day market direction classifier using an XGBoost + LightGBM ensemble — and I care a lot about the unglamorous parts: token accounting, audit logging, role-based access and cost modelling. Those are what decide whether an AI feature ships.
